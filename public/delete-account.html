Request Account and Data Deletion

Official data deletion policy for EARN24 Mobile App (Earn24 Pvt. Ltd.)

At EARN24, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play User Data policies, users can request the complete deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Method 1: Directly inside the EARN24 Mobile App
  1. Open the EARN24 App on your device and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account (located right below the Log Out button).
  4. Enter your optional remarks or reason, and tap Delete to confirm. Your account and profile will be permanently deactivated.
Method 2: Via Email Support Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team with your registered phone number or email address.

  • Recipient: support@earn24.in
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - EARN24
  • Required Details: Your registered Mobile Number and Full Name

What Data is Deleted?

Upon receiving your deletion request, EARN24 permanently removes or anonymizes the following data within 30 days:

  • Personal identification details (Name, Email address, Phone number).
  • Saved delivery addresses and shipping contacts.
  • Profile photo and login authentication credentials.
  • Shopping preferences, saved cart items, and wishlist.

What Data is Retained (If Any)?

In accordance with statutory Indian taxation, financial audit, and consumer protection laws, transactional records of past completed orders (invoices, GST records, payment transaction references) may be retained securely for statutory audit periods, after which they are permanently purged.
