EARN24 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Official Privacy Notice for EARN24 Mobile App & Website

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy outlines how Earn24 Pvt. Ltd. ("Earn24," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information of all stakeholders, including associate members, users/customers, and the company itself.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • Company Data — Information generated and maintained by Earn24 for business operations.
  • Associate Members — Data collected from distributors, delivery agents, and associates for account management, commissions, and compliance.
  • Users/Customers — Data collected during product purchases, returns, and interactions with Earn24 services on our website and mobile application.

3. Information We Collect

  • Company: Operational records, financial transactions, intellectual property, and compliance documents.
  • Associate Members: Name, contact details, government ID, banking/payment information, sales records, and performance metrics.
  • Users/Customers: Name, email address, contact phone number, delivery address, purchase history, payment transaction reference details, and customer service feedback/inquiries.

4. Use of Information

  • Company: To manage operations, ensure legal compliance, and protect intellectual property.
  • Associate Members: To process commissions, manage distributor accounts, verify identity, and enforce contractual obligations.
  • Users/Customers: To process orders, deliver products to specified shipping addresses, handle returns/refunds, provide notifications, and improve customer experience.

5. Data Protection & Security

  • All data is stored securely on protected cloud servers with restricted administrative access.
  • Industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and authentication protocols are used for digital communications and records.
  • Physical documents are safeguarded and disposed of responsibly.
  • Unauthorized disclosure, duplication, or misuse of personal data is strictly prohibited.

6. Privacy Obligations

  • Company: Complies with applicable data protection laws, including the Indian Information Technology Act (IT Act) and applicable guidelines.
  • Associate Members: Must not disclose customer identities, purchasing patterns, or personal data to unauthorized third parties.
  • Users/Customers: Must safeguard their account credentials and notify Earn24 of any suspected account misuse.

7. Sharing of Information

  • Data is shared strictly with authorized operational partners (e.g., banking partners for payment processing, logistics and delivery partners for product deliveries).
  • Earn24 does NOT sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Any sharing is strictly limited to operational necessity and governed by confidentiality agreements.

8. Retention & Disposal

  • Data is retained only as long as necessary for business, accounting, legal, or compliance purposes.
  • Upon termination of an account or verified customer request, Earn24 will delete or anonymize personal data, subject to mandatory statutory retention requirements.

9. Rights of Stakeholders

  • Associate Members & Users: Have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of their personal data by contacting support.
  • Company: Retains the right to enforce platform terms of use, verify credentials, and prevent fraud.

10. Account Deactivation & Data Deletion

Users may request account deactivation and removal of personal information at any time by contacting our support team at support@earn24.in or through the account profile section in the EARN24 mobile application.

11. Policy Updates

Earn24 may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect legal or operational changes. Continued use of EARN24 services constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
